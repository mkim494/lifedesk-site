How it works

Set up in under 5 minutes

No complicated onboarding. No manual data entry marathons. Just point, scan, and relax.

1

Add your first items

Type them in manually, scan a document with your phone camera, or let LifeDesk import from your email — renewals, warranties, subscriptions, and more.

2

LifeDesk extracts the details

Our AI reads your documents and pulls out expiration dates, renewal terms, costs, and required actions — no manual entry needed.

3

Get notified before it matters

Receive smart alerts 30, 14, and 7 days before each deadline — with specific steps tailored to your location and situation.

4

Take action in one tap

Direct links to renewal portals, comparison tools, local service providers, and money-saving alternatives — so you can handle it in minutes, not hours.
